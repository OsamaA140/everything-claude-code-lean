# Master Services Agreement — Kestrel Logistics Ltd

**1. Parties.** This Agreement is between Kestrel Logistics Ltd ("Client") and the Supplier.

**2. Services.** Supplier shall provide brand identity services: three (3) initial concepts and two (2) rounds of revision, over a ten (10) week period from kickoff.

**3. Fees.** Client shall pay Supplier a total fee of **$16,500**, invoiced in three equal instalments upon milestone completion.

**4. Payment terms.** Invoices are payable **net 60** from date of receipt.

**5. Intellectual property.** All materials, methods, tools, templates and components created or used by Supplier in connection with the Services shall be deemed **work made for hire** and shall vest in Client upon creation.

**6. Liability.** Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be capped at the total fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

**7. Indemnity.** Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless Client against any and all claims arising from the Services, **without limitation**.

**8. Term and renewal.** This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months and **renews automatically** for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives written notice not less than **ninety (90) days** before the end of the then-current term.

**9. Termination for convenience.** Client may terminate this Agreement at any time on thirty (30) days written notice. Supplier may terminate only for material breach.

**10. Governing law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of our home jurisdiction.

**11. Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep the other's confidential information confidential for three (3) years following termination.
