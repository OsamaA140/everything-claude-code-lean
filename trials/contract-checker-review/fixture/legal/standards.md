# Contract Standards

- **Payment terms:** net 30. Net 45 is the absolute maximum and needs owner sign-off.
- **Liability cap:** must be capped at fees paid in the preceding 12 months. Uncapped liability is never acceptable.
- **Auto-renewal:** acceptable only with at least 60 days notice to cancel.
- **Governing law:** our home jurisdiction. Anything else needs owner sign-off.
- **IP:** we retain ownership of our tools, methods, and reusable components. Client owns the deliverables.
- **Termination for convenience:** we must have it with 30 days notice.
