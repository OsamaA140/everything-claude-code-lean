# Past Issues — never let these recur

1. **Unlimited indemnity (Meridian, 2025).** We indemnified a client without a cap and spent four months arguing over a third-party claim. Indemnity must always be capped.
2. **Silent auto-renewal (Halcyon, 2024).** A 12-month renewal triggered because the notice window was 90 days and nobody tracked it. Never accept a notice period longer than 60 days.
3. **"Work made for hire" over our whole toolkit (Bright, 2025).** Language that assigned our reusable components to the client. Always carve out our pre-existing IP.
