# Findings — Packaging supplier alternatives
**Date: 2026-07-11**

Question: are there cheaper alternatives to BoxLine at our volume?

- **FACT** (BoxLine invoice, 2026-06-30): we pay $0.42/unit.
- **FACT** (CartonWorks published rate card, retrieved 2026-07-09): $0.39/unit at 40k volume, 25-day lead time.
- **FACT** (Verdant Pack quote by email, 2026-07-10): $0.44/unit, 12-day lead time, FSC certified.
- **INFERENCE**: CartonWorks is ~7% cheaper but a week slower. At our volume that is roughly $1,200/year saved against a lead-time risk we have not priced.
- **UNKNOWN**: none of the three were checked for compliance certification except Verdant (FSC).

Recommendation deferred to owner. No switch made.
